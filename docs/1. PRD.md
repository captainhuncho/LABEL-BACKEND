# Product Requirements

Summary only. The source of truth is the linked doc; when they disagree, the code wins, then that doc, then this file.

Full spec: [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md).

## Problem
A record label runs its roster, catalog, revenue, payouts, invoices, licences and artist relations across spreadsheets.

## Users
The label team (roles with permissions and rank rules, Product Manager scoping) and the label's artists (their own portal).

## Scope
IMSS Records — Label Operations Dashboard: roster, catalog, revenue and EMPIRE CSV imports, payouts, invoices, licences and rights, audit log, artist portal. Screens: `src/sections/`.

## Constraints
Invite only; money math lives in one place (`src/finance.js`); real data never ships in the production bundle.
