## Never

The full list is in AGENTS.md ("Never"). In short, without Max's yes for that
one action, never:

- put secrets, tokens or private links in the repo, chat, logs or commits, or
  read `.env*` files (use `.env.example`);
- delete data, databases, buckets, sign-in accounts or branches, or force-push;
- change production settings (Supabase dashboard, Vercel, DNS, cron) or push `main`;
- add third-party skills, hooks or plugins;
- write or edit legal wording (contract terms, notices, policy pages).
